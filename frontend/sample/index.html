About the Leica M3

The Leica M3, produced from 1954 to 1966, is widely regarded as one of the finest 35mm rangefinder cameras ever made. With over 220,000 units produced, it remains highly sought after by photographers and collectors alike. Its precision-machined brass body, bright 0.91× viewfinder, and reliable cloth focal-plane shutter have kept it in active use for over 70 years.

Two primary variants exist: the Double Stroke (DS), produced from 1954–1958, which requires two strokes of the film advance lever to advance one frame; and the Single Stroke (SS), produced from 1958–1966, which advances in a single motion. Both are mechanically equivalent — the DS is generally less expensive and equally reliable.
